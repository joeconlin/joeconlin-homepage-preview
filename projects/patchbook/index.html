Patchbook

A speculative reference tool for collecting studio notes and connections.

Speculative concept for review. Patchbook is an imagined studio reference app used here to demonstrate how a project page could read. The description and illustration are placeholders, not claims about a built product or its results.

Patchbook begins with a question: what if references could stay close to the small observations made while working with them?

The concept pairs a source with a brief note, then makes room to link that note to another one. The aim would be to make relationships legible without requiring a heavy cataloging ritual.

This page is deliberately a sketch. If developed, the next step would be to test the smallest useful version with people who keep working references.
